# Acme Corporation — Annual Report FY2024

## Revenue

Acme Corporation reported total revenue of $1.24 billion for fiscal year 2024, up 18% from $1.05 billion in fiscal year 2023. The growth was driven primarily by the Enterprise Software division, which contributed $620 million (50% of total revenue).

The Cloud Infrastructure segment grew 31% year over year to $310 million. The Consumer Devices business declined 4% to $180 million. Professional Services contributed the remaining $130 million.

## Profitability

Gross margin improved from 61% in FY2023 to 64% in FY2024. Operating income rose to $180 million, a 22% operating margin, compared with $140 million (13% margin) in the prior year.

Net income for FY2024 was $95 million, versus $68 million in FY2023. Diluted earnings per share (EPS) reached $1.31, up from $0.94.

## Headcount

Acme ended the year with 4,120 full-time employees, an increase of 340 employees over the prior year. The Research and Development organization grew by 22% to 1,160 employees.

## Research and Development

Acme invested $210 million in research and development during FY2024, representing 17% of revenue. Key R&D outcomes included the beta release of the Acme Assistant AI platform and the acquisition of three patents related to distributed vector search.

## Outlook for FY2025

Management guidance calls for revenue growth of 12% to 15% in fiscal year 2025, targeting a range of $1.39 billion to $1.43 billion. Operating margin is expected to remain above 20%. The company plans to launch Acme Assistant 1.0 in Q2 FY2025 and expand into the European market in Q4.

## Risk Factors

Key risks identified include concentration of revenue in the Enterprise Software segment, ongoing supply-chain constraints for consumer hardware, and increased competition in AI-enabled productivity tools.
